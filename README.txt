AI voice-over: a clear British-English female voice in a newsreader style (offline neural text-to-speech, Kokoro voice bf_emma, checked word by word with speech recognition), with burned-in English captions and a soft music bed that dips well under the voice.
Every video has captions burned in and a matching .srt file. Data is synthetic; Videos 3, 4, 5 and 9 use a demo vertical (US home insurance) that no team works on.

HOW TO PLAY: open index.html in Chrome or Edge on the presenting laptop (works offline). Click a video; F = full screen; Space = pause; N = next.
Each video also sits on its own slide in the deck as a silent, captioned clip that starts on a click: the backup if sound fails.
At every vote the video shows a countdown and a chime ("Cards up!"); pause it if a table needs longer. The scorekeeper notes the cards on the video vote log (printables).
